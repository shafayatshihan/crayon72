### ME 445 Short list of formulae (p14)
- Frequency response: $H(\omega) = \dfrac{X}{F_0} = \dfrac{1/k}{1-(\omega/\omega_n)^2 + i2\zeta\omega/\omega_n}$

- Phase: $\tan\phi = \dfrac{2\zeta(\omega/\omega_n)}{1-(\omega/\omega_n)^2}$
